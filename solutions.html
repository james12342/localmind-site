Solutions

End-to-End On-Prem AI Solutions
Designed Around Your Systems

We don’t sell a generic chatbot. We design and deploy on-premise AI platforms that plug directly into your ERP, CRM, WMS and internal tools — so your teams get real automation, not another isolated POC.

🧠

Private LLM Platform

We deploy your own LLM stack on-premise, with GPU acceleration, routing for multiple models and a clean API layer. You control the models, the hardware and the data.

  • DeepSeek / Qwen / Llama and other open-source models
  • Ollama, vLLM or SGLang inference backends
  • Support for 4090, A6000, Blackwell and more
  • Token-level logging and safety controls
🤖

AI Agent Layer

Above the models, we build reusable “agents” for support, operations, finance, logistics and more — each with its own tools, prompts and guardrails.

  • Agents tied to your databases and REST APIs
  • Role-based access to tools and knowledge bases
  • Structured outputs for downstream automation
  • Multi-step reasoning and tool planning
📊

Workflow Automation

We turn repetitive, multi-step processes into AI-powered workflows: from reading PDFs and updating records to generating reports and sending notifications.

  • Visual orchestration with conditions and loops
  • Cron-based and event-based triggers
  • Human-in-the-loop approval steps
  • Audit logs for every decision
🔌

System Integration

We sit in the middle of your stack — between front-end apps and back-office systems — so AI can read, write and coordinate across everything you already run.

  • ERP, CRM, WMS, ticketing, finance and custom tools
  • SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle and more
  • Message queues, webhooks and middleware
  • Single sign-on and role-based access

How We Work With You

We follow a structured process so that every project stays focused, measured and deliverable within weeks — not months.

1

Discovery & Use-Case Selection (1–2 weeks)

We inventory your systems and processes, then choose 2–3 high-impact workflows to tackle first. Output: written scope, success metrics, and architecture sketch.

2

On-Prem Stack Setup (1–2 weeks)

We deploy the LLM stack, vector store, orchestration layer and monitoring inside your infrastructure (or on a dedicated VPC you control).

3

Agent & Workflow Implementation (2–3 weeks)

We configure agents, connect tools and implement workflows for the agreed use cases, with test data and staging environments.

4

Launch, Training & Iteration (ongoing)

We roll out to a pilot group, collect feedback, refine prompts and workflows, and prepare documentation for a broader internal roll-out.
